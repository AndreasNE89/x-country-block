Privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026. Tamis was previously called X Country Block.

  • Tamis runs entirely in your browser. It has no servers of its own, no analytics and no account.

What Tamis reads

On x.com and twitter.com, Tamis reads data that X has already loaded into the page: the responses to X's own requests for posts and profiles, and the posts on screen. From them it uses each post's language and place tag, and for each author the handle, profile location, account language and the "Account based in" or "Connected via" line that X shows under About this account. It compares these with your picks to decide which posts to hide or highlight. It adds no flags or labels next to anyone's name. In Highlight mode, and on a matched post you open directly, it shows a short note inside the post saying why it matched, for example "Tamis · Post language: Portuguese" or the "Account based in" place X shows. These notes appear only in your own browser.

What Tamis stores, and where

Tamis stores data only in your browser's extension storage on this device (chrome.storage.local). None of it is synced or sent anywhere.

  • Your settings: the countries, regions and languages you pick, the filter mode, whether matches are highlighted instead of hidden, whether filtering is paused, and the accounts you choose to always show.
  • A cache of public profile data about accounts whose posts appeared on pages you opened: account ID, handle, profile location, "Account based in" or "Connected via", and account language. It lets matching keep working as you scroll without asking X again. It is used only for matching and is never sent anywhere. Only accounts that carry a location signal are kept, at most 5,000 of them, and each entry expires after 30 days. Nothing new is cached in private or incognito windows, or while no filter is active. Each time you open X in a normal window, Tamis trims the stored cache to these limits, even while filtering is paused or nothing is ticked. The first time you open X after updating, this also clears the larger cache kept by earlier versions (X Country Block 0.1.x). A location an account has cleared is removed.
  • Focus mode status: whether you have bought Focus mode, and when a free trial started.

Focus mode payments

Focus mode is an optional one-time payment of $5.99, made on a Stripe payment page that opens in a new tab. Stripe collects your card details, email address and billing country, and processes them under the Stripe privacy policy. Tamis never sees your card details. The developer receives the purchase record from Stripe (such as your email address, name, country, amount and date) and uses it only for the purchase, refunds and support.

After payment, Stripe returns you to this page, and Tamis records on your device that Focus mode is unlocked. There is no Tamis account, so the unlock lives only in that browser. If you reinstall Tamis, move to another browser, or the unlock was not recorded, open the Tamis popup and choose Restore. If that does not work, email us from the address you paid with and we will help you unlock it again.
